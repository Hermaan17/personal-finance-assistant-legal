3. Financial Transaction Records

The application processes text messages to identify transaction amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions. It then records the interpreted information in the application's local database.

Automated interpretation may not always be accurate. Users are responsible for reviewing transaction confirmations and checking their records in the desktop application.

The application is a recordkeeping tool and does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice.

4. User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate transaction information.

Users are responsible for securing their devices, local database files, backups, and application credentials.
